# Sources and attribution

Consulted September 19, 2026. References supply historical attribution or broad clinical background; none establishes that this game is clinically valid.

## Original inspiration

**Life and Death 2 — The Brain**, ClassicReload.
https://classicreload.com/life-and-death-2-the-brain.html

The linked page identifies the 1990 DOS game and its neurosurgeon premise. This implementation does not copy the original game binaries, artwork, music, dialogue or code, and does not reproduce its entire original case list or rules. The original game title is used descriptively. No affiliation, sponsorship or endorsement is claimed.

## Broad clinical background

**NHS — Subdural haematoma**
https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/subdural-haematoma/

Used as background for head injury, early and delayed presentations, the seriousness of intracranial bleeding and specialist assessment/treatment. The game’s invented measurements and decisions are not clinical thresholds supplied by this source.

**American Association of Neurological Surgeons — Brain Tumors**
https://www.aans.org/patients/conditions-treatments/brain-tumors/

Used as background for structural lesions, imaging, suspected tumor diagnoses and individualized care. The fictional tumor report is not a histological diagnosis.

**American Association of Neurological Surgeons — Cerebral Aneurysm**
https://www.aans.org/patients/conditions-treatments/cerebral-aneurysm/

Used as background for aneurysm rupture, subarachnoid hemorrhage and the existence of clipping and endovascular options. Historical and device-specific statements on this page were not used as current practice recommendations. The game’s clipping level is a fictional team-selected scenario.

## Not derived from clinical evidence

The initial vitals, demographic details, numerical scan measurements, target coordinates, arcade stages, arbitrary stability drain, time-out gate, scoring rubric and game success/failure conditions were created for this prototype. They have not been clinically validated.

The migraine and ischemic stroke cases are deliberately simplified fictional contrasts showing that an open operation is not always the appropriate game pathway. They do not implement complete diagnostic or therapeutic guidance, medication selection, reperfusion windows or contraindications. They require expert review before educational deployment.
